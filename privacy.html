1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Numy Hub ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website. By using the Website you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website.

This policy should be read together with our Disclaimer.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Details you submit voluntarily, such as your name and email address when you contact us, comment or subscribe.
  • Any content, files or messages you send through forms or user-submission features.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system and language.
  • Pages visited, time and date of visits, time spent on pages, referring website and approximate location (country or city level).
  • Cookie identifiers and similar technologies (see Section 4).

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as health, financial account or government ID information, and we ask that you do not submit it to us.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Website and its tools.
  • To understand how visitors use the Website and fix problems.
  • To respond to your questions, requests, copyright notices or spam reports.
  • To detect, prevent and block spam, abuse, fraud and security threats.
  • To show relevant advertising and measure its performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners may use:

Type Purpose
Essential Needed for the Website to function and stay secure.
Preference Remember settings such as theme or language.
Analytics Help us understand traffic and usage patterns.
Advertising Serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the Website work.

5. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services (for example Google Analytics) to collect information about Website usage. These services may set their own cookies and process data under their own privacy policies. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Advertising

We may display ads from third-party networks such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your browsing. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. We do not control the cookies set by third-party advertisers.

  • To protect our rights, users, or the security of the Website.
  • In connection with a merger, sale or transfer of the Website, in which case we will keep your data protected.
