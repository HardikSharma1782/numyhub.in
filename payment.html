FAQ

Payment questions.

When do I pay the 10% advance?

After we agree the plan, timeline and final price. The advance confirms your order and reserves your slot. You are never asked to pay before the scope is clear.

How is the advance calculated?

It is 10% of the agreed price for your specific service. For example, a ₹5,000 project has a ₹500 advance. Use the calculator above for any service.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared with you directly once your order is confirmed. Message me by email and I will guide you through it.

When is the remaining balance due?

The remaining amount is settled as agreed in your plan. I will state the exact timing in your quote so there are no surprises.

What about refunds?

Message me before ordering and I will explain the exact terms for your service. You can also read the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy.
